Privacy Policy

Spellbound Cinema · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Spellbound Cinema ("the app") is a video player for Meta Quest headsets. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your videos stay on your device

The app opens video and subtitle files that you choose from your headset's storage, or from a network share on your own local network that you add yourself. The app does not upload, copy or share your files.

Settings stay on your device

Preferences such as screen size, distance, brightness and the last position in a film are saved locally on your headset only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The app does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not collect or sell personal information.

Meta platform

The app uses the Meta Platform SDK only to confirm that you own the app (an "entitlement check"), as required by the Meta Horizon Store. How Meta handles your Meta account data is described in Meta's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
