Privacy Policy

Spellbound Cinema · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Spellbound Cinema ("the app", "we") is a video player for Meta Quest headsets. This policy explains exactly what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Data the app collects and how it is used

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
Your Meta user ID (app-scoped) and app entitlement status To confirm that you own the app (entitlement check), as required by the Meta Horizon Store. Processed by Meta's platform; not stored by us.
In-app purchase (IAP) information: which add-on environments you bought (product SKU), purchase ID and purchase date Only to unlock the add-ons you bought and to restore them on reinstall or on another headset. Processed by Meta's platform; a copy of the unlocked add-on list is cached on your headset.
App settings (screen size, distance, brightness, last environment, last playback position, network share addresses you add) To remember your preferences. On your headset only.

Payment details (card, billing address) are handled entirely by Meta. We never receive them.

2. Data the app does NOT collect

The app does not upload, copy or share your videos or subtitle files. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Files sent from your phone with the "From phone" feature travel only over your own local Wi-Fi to your headset. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

3. Retention

Data on your headset stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by Meta so that your purchases can be restored; see Meta's privacy policy.

4. How to delete your data

Deletion is free and available to every user, in every country.

Request deletion: email ky37194@gmail.com with the subject "Spellbound Cinema – Data deletion request". Include your Meta username so we can find your records. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

  • Data on your headset: delete it yourself at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app (Settings → Apps → Spellbound Cinema).
  • Purchase / entitlement data: after your request we delete any record we hold and ask Meta to delete the app's data for your account. You can also manage or delete your Meta account data directly in your Meta account settings (Accounts Center).

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or ask any question about it, by writing to the email address below. We answer within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
